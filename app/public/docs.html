Where the money goes

Every number on this page is readable on-chain. If one of them stops being true, the contract is wrong, not the page.

What you pay

You sell your dust for ETH. The contract keeps 5% of what it sold for. That is MAX_FEE_BPS, a hardcoded ceiling, so the fee cannot be raised above it by anyone, including us. It can be lowered.

The gas is on you. It comes out of your ETH balance, not out of the proceeds, and the site shows you the estimate before you sign. Each token also needs a one-off approval the first time you sweep it. Quotes carry a 3% haircut before they are compared to gas, so a token that only barely clears is left in the wallet rather than sold at a loss.

Where the 5% lands

Right now, all of it goes to the dev wallet:

0x5dCD1D1DD0F797a24Cc509fDd0Df9e8747bBD01b

That is a hardware wallet. It is also the contract owner. Owner can change fees (down to zero, up to 5%), register or remove swap adapters, and set the payout token. Owner cannot touch your tokens: the contract never holds a balance and every approval is exact-amount, per sweep.

Once $SWEEP exists and a buyback contract is live, the split becomes:

2.5%buys $SWEEP on-chain and burns it. Automated, visible, no discretion.
2.5%pays the dev. One person, building in public.

Until that contract is deployed and this page says so, the honest description is the one above: 100% dev. We would rather write that than promise a split that is not wired yet.

What $SWEEP has to do with it

$SWEEP is the companion token, launched on the Pons launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Pons pays the creator of a token 70% of a 1% fee on every trade of that token, for as long as it trades. That stream goes 100% to the dev.

So, plainly: the sweeper is not the business. The sweeper is the reason anyone hears about the token, and the token's trading volume is what pays for the sweeper to exist. You are welcome to use the tool and never touch the token.

Paying out in $SWEEP instead of ETH is a feature the contract supports but the site does not offer yet. It turns on once the pool has real depth and the path has been tested against it.

What we do not do

No custody. Your tokens go from your wallet through the contract to a pool in one transaction, and the ETH comes back to you in the same one. No address logging, no analytics on who connected. No token gets sold that you did not tick.

Unaudited. The contract source is public at github.com/hellojakejohn/dustsweep.xyz and it interacts with arbitrary tokens, some of which are hostile by design. It has been fork-tested against real dead tokens, run through static analysis (Aderyn), and used on the dev's own wallet first. That is not the same as an audit. Read it, or sweep an amount you would not miss.
